# How to talk about it: The Magic Pebble

**Why this topic at 5–7.** This episode introduces Emma, Grandma and Pietrolino. It has only one message: you notice things when you stop. At this age stopping is hard, and that is exactly why it can become an interesting game.

## Three questions after the story

1. Why did Pietrolino talk to Emma?
2. Where was Grandma while Emma was playing by the stream?
3. If you stop and stay quiet, what can you hear right now?

## In real life

Try the "listening minute" together: in the garden, at the park or at the window, eyes closed, everyone counts on their fingers the sounds they hear. Then you tell each other. You don't need a wood: a balcony will do.

## Common mistakes

- Explaining straight away "what the story means": let the child get there through the questions.
- Turning listening into a test ("didn't you hear anything?").
- Loading the child with the job of "saving the world": here we are only starting to listen.
