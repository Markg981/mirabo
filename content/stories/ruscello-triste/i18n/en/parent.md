# How to talk about it: The Sad Little Stream

**Why this topic at 6–7.** At this age children notice litter in nature and are often more upset by it than adults; the story gives them a possible action and an adult who makes it safe, so the upset becomes action instead of anxiety.

## Three questions after the story

1. Why couldn't the stream sing any more?
2. Why did Emma go to Grandma before starting?
3. Have you ever seen a place that needed help?

## In real life

Keep gloves and a bag at home, so the idea can be put into practice straight away; agree together on what must not be touched (glass, needles, things you don't recognise, animals); let the child be the one to tell what they did, without telling it for them.

## Common mistakes

- Saying "people are so rude": it turns the topic into anger towards others.
- Doing everything for them.
- Turning the walk into a weekly chore.
