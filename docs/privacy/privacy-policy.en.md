# Mirabò Privacy Notice (for parents)

Last updated: [publication date, to fill in]

Mirabò is an app of stories and small steps for children aged 3 to 10. This notice is written for parents. References: GDPR (Reg. EU 2016/679) arts. 12–14 and 8; for the United States 16 CFR Part 312 (COPPA). Analysis: `docs/privacy/coppa.md`.

## 2. In short

- Mirabò is free, has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and creates no account for the child.
- It works offline. Profile data stays **on your device**, in an encrypted database.
- We do not receive your children's data on any server: we have none.
- Only if you turn it on, from the parent area, you can back up to **your own** Google Drive.

## 3. What data and why

Data stays on the device, except as described in section 4.

| Data | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per profile: name or nickname you choose, age band, language, chosen companion, settings (mode, high-legibility text, "on my own" minutes) | To make the app work for that child | Performance of the service requested by the parent (art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Stories read and finished, dialogue answers, activity rounds and progress, gestures marked, invented stories, nature calendar | To resume where you left off and prepare the parent diary | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Usage time per day and child | To show you how long the app is used | Art. 6(1)(b); on the device, not transmitted |
| Free notes you write in the diary | A memory aid for the parent | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Child's name spoken by the system voice, stored in the local cache | To call the child by name | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Drive backup (section 4) | To restore data if you change device | **Consent** of the parent (art. 6(1)(a)), withdrawable |
| Printing and sharing PDFs | When you choose, the PDF goes to the printer or the app you pick | Art. 6(1)(b) (parent's choice) |

Processing that stays on the device with no access by the controller may fall under the parent's personal use: **to verify** with counsel whether and to what extent the controller is still considered one.

We do not collect: location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers, or data for profiling or marketing. There is no automated decision-making.

## 4. Google Drive backup (optional)

- It is turned on only from the parent area, behind the adult check, with **your** Google account.
- The permission requested is `drive.appdata`: the app sees only its own hidden folder, not the rest of your Drive.
- The file holds profiles, diary, progress and activities, and is **encrypted** (AES-256-GCM) with a password you choose. Neither we nor Google can read it; without the password it cannot be recovered.
- On the device, the secure storage keeps the derived key, the account address and the date of the last backup.
- Google processes data as a controller for its own service (Google's notice applies).
- You can turn it off at any time: the device forgets account and key and revokes the permission. You keep or delete the Drive copy from Drive › Settings › Manage apps.
- Legal basis: consent, art. 6(1)(a). Declining removes no app feature.

## 5. How long we keep data

On the device until you delete it (*Delete all data* or uninstalling the app). The Drive backup stays until you delete it; each backup replaces the previous one. We keep no copies. [Define a retention period in the processing register if controller-side collection is ever added.]

## 6. Who we share data with

Nobody, except Google (only if you enable backup) and the printer or app you choose for PDFs. We do not sell data.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and to withdraw consent (GDPR arts. 15–21 and 7(3)). You can exercise them right in the app: edit or delete profiles, *Delete all data*, turn off backup. For any request: [controller email, to fill in]. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it) or your local authority.

## 8. Children

The app is designed for children aged 3–10 but is **set up and managed by the parent**: the child has no account and enters no personal data. In Italy a child's own digital consent is valid from age 14 (GDPR art. 8 and art. 2-quinquies of the Privacy Code): below 14, whoever holds parental responsibility decides. For the United States, see `docs/privacy/coppa.md`. The adult check in the app is a barrier against accidental use by the child and does **not** verify the parent's identity.

## 9. Security

Encrypted on-device database (SQLCipher), key in the system secure storage, signed story packages. No system is perfectly secure.

## 10. Changes

If our practices change (for example with a cloud account), we will update this notice and ask for consent where needed.
