# Mirabò Privacy Notice (for parents)

Last updated: [publication date, to fill in]

Mirabò is an app of stories and small steps for children aged 3 to 10. This notice is written for parents. References: GDPR (Reg. EU 2016/679) arts. 12–14 and 8. Technical details: `docs/privacy/mvp-data.md`.

## 1. Who we are

Data controller: [name or company, address, contact email: to fill in].
Data Protection Officer: [not appointed / to assess with counsel].

## 2. In short

- Mirabò is free, has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and creates no account for your child or for you.
- It works offline and, on Android, does not even have permission to use the network. Data stays **on your device**, in an encrypted database.
- We do not receive your children's data: we have no servers that collect it.
- The app does not keep attempts, mistakes, hints used, scores, or your child's answers to the questions in the stories.

## 3. What data and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis (GDPR, to be validated) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| For each profile: name or nickname chosen by you, age band, language, chosen companion | Recognise the profile and offer suitable content | Performance of the service requested by the parent (art. 6.1.b) |
| Settings: enabled worlds, easy-to-read text, low stimulation, "on my own" limit, interaction profile, shelf | Adapt the app as you chose | Art. 6.1.b |
| Where you left off: story screen, drawing, chosen level in each activity | Resume where you stopped | Art. 6.1.b |
| Stories read and activities done (which and when), minutes per day with the app open | The diary and the week in the parent area; knowing how much the app is used | Art. 6.1.b; on the device and not transmitted |
| What you write or mark: notes, real-life gestures, small-project notes, invented stories, nature calendar | Family memory | Art. 6.1.b |
| Parent area PIN and recovery code, kept only as a hash in secure storage | Keep the child out of the parent area | Art. 6.1.b |
| Printing and sharing PDFs | When you choose, the generated PDF goes to the printer or app you pick | Art. 6.1.b (parent's choice) |

Your child's name is not spoken by the app's voice and is not sent to the device's speech engine.

Processing only on the device, without access by the controller, may fall under the parent's personal use: **to be verified** with counsel whether and to what extent the controller is still considered as such.

We do not collect: location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers, data for profiling or marketing, performance indicators about your child. There is no automated decision-making.

## 4. No online backup

This version makes no online backups: data stays only on the device and is lost if the app is uninstalled. It is not included in system backups (iCloud, Google) either. If a previous version had a backup on your Google Drive, the app has forgotten the account and key; the encrypted copy stays in your Drive until you delete it from Drive › Settings › Manage apps.

## 5. How long we keep data

On the device until you delete it: deleting a profile removes its data and settings without touching other profiles; *Delete all data* takes the app back to its first start (including the PIN); uninstalling removes everything. We keep no copies.

## 6. Who we share data with

No one, except the printer or app you choose for PDFs. We do not sell data.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (GDPR arts. 15–21). You can exercise them in the app right away: edit or delete profiles, *Delete all data*. For any request: [controller email, to fill in]. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

## 8. Children

The app is designed for children aged 3–10 but is **set up and managed by the parent**: the child has no account and enters no personal data. In Italy a child's own digital consent is valid from age 14 (GDPR art. 8 and art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code): below 14, the holder of parental responsibility decides. The parent area PIN keeps the child out and does **not** verify the parent's identity: it is not verified parental consent. The app is not distributed in the United States: this is a distribution choice, not a statement of compliance with US law (COPPA).

## 9. Security

Encrypted database on the device (SQLCipher), key in the system's secure storage, signed story packages. No system is one hundred percent secure.

## 10. Changes

If we change our practices (for example with an online backup or an account), we will update this notice and ask for your consent where needed.
